## What each dataset does in the system

No code reads these files yet; this is the role each one is planned to play. Every value that
reaches the app carries a `provenance` label: `real` (taken directly from a dataset), `replayed`
(real data re-timed onto a simulated mission), or `simulated` (generated by `generator/sim.py`).

### 1. ISS cabin CO₂ (Environmental Data App): environment signal

- **System:** sets the normal range for `cabin_co2_mmhg`. Today the simulator assumes a mean of
  2.0 mmHg; the real ISS readings replace that guess with the station's actual level and
  day-to-day variation.
- **Demo:** the generator can replay real CO₂ days (`replayed`) before the "CO₂ creep" scenario
  pushes it up, so the baseline the rule engine learns is a real one.
- **AI:** the autoencoder's CO₂ input is trained on realistic normal variation, so a real cabin's
  ordinary noise does not trigger a false anomaly.

### 2. RadLab ISS dose rate: radiation signal and station preset

- **System:** gives the Station preset its dose rate (plan 4.4), derived by averaging
  the µGy/hour readings into a daily dose. That daily dose then accumulates
  into the cumulative-dose signal that the rule engine compares to the mission dose budget.
- **Demo:** this is the "real NASA data runs through the system" proof (plan 5.1): the
  radiation readings in the app are replayed ISS measurements, not invented ones.
- **AI:** one input to the immune-risk score (radiation dose is one of its weighted terms,
  plan 4.3 step 5). It is not an autoencoder input.

### 3. Curiosity RAD (published values): Mars presets

- **System:** the Mars transit (0.48 mGy/day) and Mars surface (0.21 mGy/day) presets use these
  numbers directly, so switching preset changes how fast dose accumulates.
- **AI:** none.

### 4. PhysioNet BIDSleep: wearable vitals and sleep

- **System:** calibrates the simulator. Per-night resting heart rate, sleep duration and sleep
  efficiency (from the EEG-scored sleep stages) give real values for each signal's typical
  level, its night-to-night variation (`sim_sd`), and how much people differ from each other
  (`person_sd`) in `contract/signals.yaml`.
- **Demo:** a real night's heart-rate stream can drive the pulse signal (`replayed`).
- **AI:** the main training-data anchor. The autoencoder learns "normal" from simulated days
  shaped to match these 253 real nights, so its idea of normal day-to-day variation for
  heart rate and sleep comes from real people rather than our guesses. Evaluation should split
  by subject, not by night, so the model is never tested on a person it trained on.
- **Limit:** these are healthy adults on Earth, not astronauts. It anchors normal variation,
  not spaceflight effects.

### 5. Inspiration4 clinical panels (OSDR): lab analyzer signals

- **System:** sets realistic values for the lab-analyzer signals (plan 4.1, immune and metabolic
  rows): lymphocyte counts from the blood count, glucose and other chemistry from the metabolic
  panel, cytokines from the immune panels. The analyzer chip and generator publish values in
  these real ranges and units.
- **Demo:** before → in-flight → after measurements from a real crew show the direction and
  size of spaceflight changes, which the immune scenarios can copy.
- **AI:** anchors the immune-risk score. The real crew's spread tells us what counts as a
  meaningful deviation for each immune input, instead of picking weights from nothing.
- **Limit:** 4 people, a 3-day flight, a few time points. Good for ranges and direction,
  not for training a model.

### 6. NOAA SWPC proton flux: solar particle events

- **System:** optional. A spike in real proton flux can trigger a solar-particle-event
  scenario: dose rate jumps, and the radiation system goes amber or red.
- **AI:** none.

## Notes

- EDA CO2 is in ppm: divide by ~1316 for mmHg (at 1 atm). Median across RR-3 is ~3600 ppm ≈ 2.8 mmHg.
  The first readings of a mission can sit at a flat 2000.0, which looks like a fill value: drop
  flat runs before computing a baseline. The series is labelled ISS (vs ground control) but comes
  from rodent-habitat missions: confirm it is cabin air, not inside the habitat.
- RadLab DosTel coverage ends mid-April 2022; earlier windows (2019–2021) also work if more history is needed.
- BIDSleep: cite the dataset and Song et al. (IEEE TBME 2025); split by subject, not night. Check its licence file in `data/raw/bidsleep/`.
- RadLab API syntax: `?spacecraft=ISS&instrument=DosTel&timestamp%3E=...&timestamp%3C...&<column>&format=csv`. Bare key = return column, `key=value` = filter.

## Gaps (simulated, labelled `provenance: simulated`)

Beat-to-beat in-flight HRV, reaction-test results, mood: no open astronaut data.
